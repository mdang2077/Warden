Warden

About Warden

Warden blocks known phishing pages and the pop-ups and redirects that lead to them. It runs on your device; nothing about your browsing leaves it except 4-byte hash prefixes for Safe Browsing lookups.

Phishing data

Warden checks the pages you open against Google Safe Browsing. Google works to provide the most accurate and up-to-date information about unsafe web resources. However, Google cannot guarantee that its information is comprehensive and error-free: some risky sites may not be identified, and some safe sites may be identified in error. The same is true of Warden's own checks: they can miss phishing pages and, rarely, flag safe ones.
